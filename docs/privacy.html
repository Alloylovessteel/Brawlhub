BrawlHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BrawlHub handles when you use its Discord bot, website and related services. BrawlHub is not affiliated with Supercell or Discord.

1. Information we handle

Depending on how you use BrawlHub, we may handle:

  • Discord information: Discord user ID, guild ID, channel ID and permission-related role information needed to operate commands and authorisations.
  • Brawl Stars information: player tags, club tags, names, trophies, club roles, badges, descriptions and other fields returned by the official Brawl Stars API.
  • Membership history: club join, leave and transfer events derived from synced roster data.
  • Competition data: competition names, rules, entries, scores, submission records and approval status.
  • Match aggregates: where enabled, daily/weekly/seasonal counts derived from official battle-log data, such as matches played, wins, draws and trophy change. Raw battle logs are not intended to be retained as a permanent record.
  • Settings and audit records: supported guild settings, role grants, blacklist records and administrative audit events.
  • Website authentication data: session information managed through Supabase Auth when you sign in to the web dashboard.

2. Sources of information

We obtain information from:

  • you, when you run commands or use the website;
  • Discord, when you authorise the bot or interact with it;
  • the official Brawl Stars API, for public player and club data;
  • BrawlHub’s own scheduled synchronisation and stored snapshots.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • display club, player, leaderboard and history information;
  • verify membership and maintain role-based permissions;
  • run competitions and calculate standings;
  • prevent abuse, duplications and unauthorised administrative actions;
  • maintain audit logs for security and troubleshooting.

4. Sharing

BrawlHub does not sell personal information. Data is processed by service providers needed to operate the project, including Discord, Supabase and hosting providers. Public statistics displayed in a Discord server may be visible to members of that server. We may disclose information only where required by law or necessary to protect users and service integrity.

5. Retention

Information is retained only as long as needed for BrawlHub’s operation, security and history features. Aggregated statistics are preferred over retaining raw event payloads. Historical snapshots, membership events and audit records may remain available to support history, moderation and troubleshooting. Removal requests can be reviewed subject to legal, security and technical constraints.

6. Cookies and sessions

The website may use authentication cookies or session data provided by Supabase Auth. The Discord bot does not use browser cookies.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of information BrawlHub holds about you by contacting the BrawlHub owner. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You can stop using BrawlHub at any time and may remove the bot from a Discord server.

8. Children

BrawlHub is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through BrawlHub, contact the owner so it can be reviewed.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as BrawlHub changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Contact the BrawlHub owner through Discord or a non-sensitive support request in the project repository. Do not include API keys, tokens, passwords or other credentials.
